# Mekh - Automotive Services Marketplace

> Kenya's #1 Automotive Services Marketplace connecting vehicle owners with trusted, verified automotive technicians.

## About Us
Mekh is a platform where users can find and book top-rated automotive technicians in Nairobi, Mombasa, and across Kenya. We offer a wide range of services including:
- Window Tinting
- Ceramic Coating
- PPF (Paint Protection Film)
- Car Audio & Electronics
- Roadside Emergency Services
- General Mechanic Services
